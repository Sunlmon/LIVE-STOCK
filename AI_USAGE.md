# AI Usage Disclosure

**Project:** LIVE STOCK. Initially compiled on 29 October 2026; reviewed and translated into English on 31 October 2026.

This account was prepared with AI assistance from the author's development conversations, project code and verification records. AI contributed substantially to code generation, revision, debugging and documentation, rather than only proofreading. The author should check that it accurately describes their work before submission.

## Tools

The main tools were **ChatGPT / Codex**, used for discussion, implementation and checking. Exact model versions, invocation times and usage were not fully recorded, so this account does not invent model names, hours or contribution percentages. No external AI service is integrated into gameplay.

## One suggestion retained

After preparing A and B icon previews, AI recommended the more distinctive B direction for small-size recognition. I retained its cat, dog, rabbit, hamster and goldfish designs. Pointed ears, drooping ears, long ears, full cheeks and a complete fish silhouette better served my aim than changing expressions alone. Production assets were replaced only after I approved the previews.

## One suggestion modified

I did not accept the entire B set. I specifically selected the A parrot, producing a mixed set of five B icons and one A icon. The final visual choice was mine.

I also repeatedly revised early AI-assisted breeding effects that shrank parents into black dots, introduced offspring abruptly, or became stuck. I required the parent to remain unchanged while offspring moved continuously from its centre. The completion page changed from an eight-second automatic transition, originally requested by me, to manual View results. That was a change in my requirements, not an invented example of rejecting an AI-originated proposal.

## Runtime and player data

AI is a development aid, not a game component. Matter.js computes physical motion; a separate creative simulation assigns outcomes to the unadopted snapshot. Players need no AI account. The game does not send names, clicks, outlet records or session credentials to AI services. Only language preference is stored locally in `localStorage`.
